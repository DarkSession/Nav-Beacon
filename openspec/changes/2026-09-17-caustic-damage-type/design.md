## Screens

The change introduces no screen. It adds one legend line and one bar segment to the damage-by-type
reading the offence analysis canvas already composes, and those follow the shape of the five lines
beside them.

## The caustic segment's ground

The bar draws each conventional type on its own semantic token. Caustic takes
`--ednb-surface-damage-caustic`, bound to the brighter green primitive beside the healthy green that
explosive already uses. Six grounds cannot all be far apart in hue on one dark theme, so two pairs
sit close: kinetic beside thermal, and explosive beside caustic.

That is acceptable because the colour is not the reading. Every segment states its own type, its
exact amount and its share in the legend line beside it, so a Commander who cannot tell the two
greens apart still reads both rows. The alternative — a sixth distinct hue — would either leave the
palette or weaken a ground that already carries a meaning elsewhere in the theme.

## What the unclassified line becomes

`unclassified` stays in the projection. The package still declares it, and a later catalogue may
deal it again. What changes is the evidence available: no 0.2.13 article deals it, so the
package-acceptance suite can pin its absence but cannot pin an amount. The fixture that formerly
produced an unclassified amount now produces the caustic one, and the suite says in a comment that
the absence is all it can pin.
